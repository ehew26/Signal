# Vertex AI Consulting — Executive Growth Plan

*A six-function operating plan: CEO, Sales, Marketing, Operations, Client
Success, CFO. Target market: small businesses with 5–100 employees. Mission:
implement AI + automation that grows revenue, cuts labor cost, and improves
customer experience.*

> This plan is grounded in what's already built: a Next.js site on Vercel with
> self-serve Stripe subscriptions — **Starter $299/mo**, **Growth $599/mo**,
> **Done-For-You (custom)** — automated onboarding email, and a billing portal.

---

## 1. Each executive's top 10 highest-impact actions

### Agent 1 — CEO (strategy, positioning, growth)
1. **Niche down to ONE vertical first** (recommend: home services — HVAC,
   plumbing, elect, roofing, med spas). Specificity wins small-business trust.
2. Reposition from "AI consulting" to a **revenue outcome**: "We make sure you
   never miss another customer call or lead."
3. Lead with the **missed-call / missed-lead problem** — universally felt, easy
   to quantify in dollars.
4. Set a single 12-month goal: **$50k MRR** (~85 Starter-equivalent clients or
   a blended mix).
5. Build a **referral-and-results flywheel**, not an ad-dependent business.
6. Productize hard — no custom snowflakes below the Done-For-You tier.
7. Pick **3 KPIs** that matter: MRR, churn, CAC payback. Ignore vanity metrics.
8. Create a **partner channel** (web designers, MSPs, marketing agencies who
   resell to their SMB books).
9. Keep delivery near-zero-touch so margins stay >70%.
10. Re-invest first $10k MRR into one growth hire (SDR/closer) + content.

### Agent 2 — Sales Director (leads, lists, outreach, closing)
1. Build a **target list of 500 local SMBs** in the chosen vertical (Google
   Maps + Apollo/Apify scrape).
2. Run the **"secret shopper" hook**: call/fill their form, let it go
   unanswered, then show them the missed lead. Devastatingly effective.
3. Offer a **free "Lead Leak Audit"** as the top-of-funnel CTA, not a demo.
4. Cold email + cold call + LinkedIn — **3-touch** sequences, 50 prospects/day.
5. Use a **free trial / 14-day pilot** to kill purchase friction on Starter.
6. Book **discovery calls**, not "demos" — diagnose dollar leakage first.
7. Sell the **Growth plan as the default** (anchor with Done-For-You above it).
8. Close on the call with a **Stripe checkout link** sent live.
9. Ask every closed client for **2 referrals** at the onboarding high.
10. Track everything in a simple CRM; **follow up 5+ times** (most sales are 5th+ touch).

### Agent 3 — Marketing Director (copy, content, case studies, authority)
1. Rewrite homepage hero around the **niche + dollar outcome**.
2. Publish a **"How much money your missed calls cost you" calculator** (lead magnet).
3. Ship **3 founder case studies** with real $ numbers ASAP (even pilot results).
4. Post **short vertical-specific content** daily on LinkedIn + local Facebook groups.
5. Create a **before/after demo video** of the AI answering a missed call.
6. Collect **video testimonials** — the #1 SMB trust asset.
7. Build a **Google Business Profile + reviews** for Vertex itself (practice what you sell).
8. Run **retargeting ads** to audit-page visitors only (cheap, high intent).
9. Repurpose every case study into email, LinkedIn, and a YouTube short.
10. Own one phrase: **"Never miss a customer again."**

### Agent 4 — Operations Director (SOPs, PM, automation, delivery)
1. Write a **one-page onboarding SOP** (intake → config → go-live in 48h).
2. Build the **standard tech stack once**, reuse per client (see §13).
3. Create an **intake form** that captures everything needed to configure in one pass.
4. Automate **provisioning**: webhook → create client workspace → send setup link.
5. Template the **AI assistant prompts** per vertical (swap business details only).
6. Set up **monitoring + alerting** so failures are caught before clients notice.
7. Maintain a **runbook** for the 10 most common support issues.
8. Use **one PM board** (Notion/Trello) with a fixed pipeline per client.
9. Define **SLAs**: go-live ≤48h, support response ≤4 business hours.
10. Track **time-per-client**; drive setup labor toward <2 hours.

### Agent 5 — Client Success Director (onboarding, comms, reporting, retention)
1. Send the **automated welcome email** (built) + a 10-min kickoff call.
2. Deliver a **"first win" in week 1** (a captured lead they'd have missed).
3. Send a **monthly results report**: leads captured, calls answered, $ saved.
4. Run a **30-day check-in** to convert Starter → Growth.
5. Make **cancellation self-serve** but add a "pause" + win-back offer.
6. Build a **referral ask** into the 60-day touchpoint.
7. Watch a **health score** (usage + results) to predict churn.
8. Keep a **shared channel** (SMS/email) for instant client questions.
9. Celebrate **milestones** ("you've captured 100 leads!") to deepen stickiness.
10. Target **<5% monthly churn**; every retained client compounds MRR.

### Agent 6 — CFO (pricing, margins, projections, profitability)
1. Add a **one-time setup fee ($500–$1,500)** to fund CAC and qualify buyers.
2. Offer **annual plans at 2 months free** to boost cash + cut churn.
3. Keep **gross margin >75%** — software-led delivery, not labor-led.
4. Cap **CAC payback at <2 months** (setup fee should nearly cover it).
5. Introduce **usage-based add-ons** (extra locations, call volume) for expansion revenue.
6. Model **3 scenarios** (conservative/base/aggressive) — see §6.
7. Hold **3 months operating cash** before any hire.
8. Price the **Done-For-You tier at $2,500–$5,000 setup + $999+/mo**.
9. Track **MRR, churn, LTV:CAC (target >4:1)** weekly.
10. Reinvest **40% of gross profit** into growth until $50k MRR.

## 2. Cross-functional debate (the real tensions)

- **Free trial vs. setup fee** (Sales ⚔ CFO). *Resolution:* charge a setup fee
  but make it **100% refundable if not live in 48h** — removes risk without
  giving away margin. Pilot pricing only for the first 5 case-study clients.
- **Niche down vs. take any client** (CEO ⚔ Sales hunger). *Resolution:* market
  and message to **one vertical**, but accept adjacent inbound. Niche is a
  marketing decision, not a refusal policy.
- **Custom work vs. productized** (Sales ⚔ Ops). *Resolution:* anything custom
  goes into the **Done-For-You tier at Done-For-You pricing**. Starter/Growth
  are rigid by design.
- **Speed to cash vs. retention** (Sales ⚔ Client Success). *Resolution:*
  comp/celebrate on **retained** revenue, not just closed — align incentives to
  the 90-day mark, not signature day.
- **Ad spend vs. organic** (Marketing ⚔ CFO). *Resolution:* **no paid ads until
  $10k MRR** except cheap retargeting of audit-page visitors. Outbound +
  referrals fund the early months.

## 5. Time-boxed plans

### 30-DAY PLAN — "First dollars" (goal: 3–5 paying clients, ~$1.5–3k MRR)
- **Week 1:** Lock the vertical. Rewrite homepage hero + add "Lead Leak Audit"
  CTA. Build the 500-prospect list. Set up CRM + outbound tooling.
- **Week 2:** Launch outbound (50 prospects/day). Record the demo video. Run
  secret-shopper on top 50 targets. Book 10+ discovery calls.
- **Week 3:** Close first 3–5 clients at **pilot pricing** in exchange for case
  studies. Onboard them in 48h. Deliver a week-1 win.
- **Week 4:** Publish first case study. Ask each client for 2 referrals. Tune
  the script based on objections heard. Turn on Stripe live keys.

### 90-DAY PLAN — "Repeatable engine" (goal: ~$8–12k MRR, 15–25 clients)
- Outbound running daily; **20%+ of new clients from referrals**.
- 3 case studies + 3 video testimonials published.
- Setup fee + annual plans live; Growth is the default sell.
- Onboarding fully templated; setup labor <2 hrs/client.
- Monthly results reports automated; churn <5%.
- Start daily LinkedIn content + retargeting on audit visitors.
- Sign first 1–2 **reseller partners**.

### 1-YEAR PLAN — "Scale" (goal: ~$50k MRR, ~85–110 clients blended)
- Hire 1 closer + 1 part-time ops/CS.
- Expand to a **second vertical** using the proven playbook.
- Launch usage-based add-ons (multi-location, higher call volume).
- Partner channel contributing 30%+ of new logos.
- LTV:CAC >4:1, gross margin >75%, 3 months cash in bank.
- Optional: paid ads once unit economics are proven.

## 10. Sales scripts

**Cold call (secret-shopper open):**
> "Hi, is this [Name]? I'm local — quick heads up, not a pitch: I tried to reach
> [Business] yesterday after hours and the call went unanswered. I help [HVAC]
> companies make sure that never happens — every missed call gets an instant
> text back and the lead's captured automatically. If I showed you exactly how
> many calls you're missing, would that be worth 10 minutes?"

**Cold email:**
> **Subject:** the call [Business] missed yesterday
> [Name] — I called [Business] after 6pm yesterday and got voicemail. For a
> [trade] business that's usually a $300–$3,000 job walking to a competitor.
> We set up an AI that answers/texts back every missed call and books the job —
> live in 48 hours, done for you. Worth a quick look? I'll send a free Lead Leak
> Audit showing what it's costing you. — [You]

**Discovery → close:**
> "So you're getting ~[X] calls/month, missing ~[Y], average job ~$[Z]. That's
> roughly **$[Y×Z] walking out the door every month.** Growth is $599/mo and
> pays for itself with **one** saved job. I can have you live by [day]. Want me
> to send the checkout link now so we lock in your setup slot this week?"

**Referral ask (day 30):**
> "Glad it's working — you've captured [N] leads already. Who's one other
> [trade] owner you know who'd hate losing calls as much as you did? Happy to
> give them the same setup."

## 11. SOPs (one-pagers to write)
1. **Lead → Close SOP:** list build → 3-touch sequence → discovery → Stripe link
   → won. Logged in CRM with 5+ follow-ups.
2. **Onboarding SOP (48h):** payment webhook → welcome email (auto) → intake
   form → configure assistant from vertical template → test call → go-live →
   kickoff message.
3. **Delivery/Config SOP:** clone standard stack → fill business details →
   connect phone/forms → QA checklist → monitor.
4. **Support SOP:** triage by runbook → resolve common-10 → escalate → log.
5. **Reporting SOP:** monthly auto-report (leads, calls, $ saved) → 30/60/90
   touchpoints → upsell + referral asks.
6. **Win-back SOP:** cancellation → offer pause + discount → exit survey.

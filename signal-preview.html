The Process

Dating, the
way it should
feel.

We built Signal because app culture broke dating. Infinite choices create decision paralysis. Verification removes bad actors. Voice profiles reveal personality. And one weekly drop restores intention.

01

Get verified

Every Signal member completes identity verification via biometric selfie. Your verified badge signals you're real — and so is everyone you'll meet.

02

Build your voice profile

Record three 30-second voice prompts. Let people hear your laugh, your cadence, your personality — before they ever swipe right.

03

Receive your five

Every Monday at 7am, five curated matches arrive. We've already filtered for location, age, and compatibility. You just decide who feels right.

04

Signal. Match. Meet.

Send a signal to people you're interested in. When it's mutual, you match. The Compact keeps both people accountable — respond within 72 hours.

## PSYCHOLOGY TACTICS THAT WILL WORK

1. **Founder energy** — "I built this because I needed it" converts better than ads. Keep it personal.
2. **Scarcity is real** — 150 spots IS the cap. Say it every post. 
3. **Local identity** — Sarasotans have pride. "This is ours" is powerful.
4. **Voice prompts** — That's the differentiator no other app has locally. Lead with it in demos.
5. **Anti-Hinge framing** — People already hate the big apps. Position against them.

## TIKTOK VIDEO PRODUCTION NOTES

### Equipment needed:
- Smartphone (portrait mode, good lighting)
- Ring light or face a window (natural light = authentic)
- Clean or minimalist background (white wall, outside in Sarasota)

### Filming tips:
- Don't overthink — raw/authentic outperforms produced on TikTok
- Film multiple takes, pick the one where you look most natural
- Show the phone screen for product shots (screen record + cut in)
- Add captions (TikTok auto-captions work)

### Audio:
- Speak clearly, no background music on talking videos (TikTok speech clarity)
- Can add trending audio under at 5-10% volume if needed

### Editing:
- Use TikTok's native editor
- Keep cuts tight — no dead air
- Text overlays for key points
- End card always: signal-date-app.vercel.app
